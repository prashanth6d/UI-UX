Executive Summary

The updated 6D Technologies Case Management flow represents a meaningful and well-structured iteration over the baseline. The design team has addressed several core usability concerns from the prior review — most notably the AI Investigation lifecycle clarity, the Transfer Case modal's information completeness, and the introduction of a Knowledge Base Playbook with step-level completion tracking. The overall UX quality has improved from 7.1 to 8.2 out of 10, a gain of +1.1 points.

Standout improvements include the AI Investigation complete state with a visible confidence score (94%) and clearly structured root cause findings, the live call integration with AI Live Notes auto-capture, the CRM 360° enterprise intelligence layer with full customer profiling, and the Knowledge Base Playbook with a "Mark Complete" interaction per step. The Transfer Case modal now surfaces team availability, SLA impact, customer notification options, and system action declarations — removing the opacity that was the primary concern in the baseline review.

The platform is approaching production-ready enterprise quality. Remaining gaps are focused in three areas: accessibility compliance, a missing Resolve Case confirmation guardrail, and label/heading copy inconsistencies (notably "Issue Summery" — a persistent misspelling across screens). Addressing these will position the platform as a benchmark enterprise case management experience.

Remaining UX Gaps
!
"Issue Summery" heading typo — present across all case screens Critical
The label "Issue Summery" appears consistently across every case detail screen (pages 3–12, 18). This is a misspelling of "Summary" and is visible to agents and customers alike. In a product presented to enterprise clients, this is a trust and quality signal failure that must be corrected before any external demonstration.
!
No Resolve Case confirmation dialog Critical
The "Resolve" button is a primary CTA present on every case detail screen. Case resolution is irreversible and triggers downstream events — SLA logging, customer notification, case closure. No confirmation step or undo affordance is visible. This is a high-risk gap for enterprise compliance workflows.
!
AI Investigation finding language remains probabilistic Critical
Despite the 94% confidence score, the AI recommended actions do not include a direct escalation CTA. Agents must manually interpret the recommended actions and initiate the transfer independently. For a confirmed system error, this should surface a single deterministic "Escalate to Technical Support" action button.
~
Duplicate contact data in CRM 360° Profile Info Moderate
Contact 1 and Contact 2 show identical name, email, and phone (Robert Robertson / Davidjo9086@gmail.com / 890867890). Similarly, Current Address 1 and Address 2 are identical. This appears to be placeholder or incorrect data — a trust and data quality concern for enterprise demos and real deployments.
~
Accessibility gaps — WCAG 2.1 AA compliance risk Moderate
No visible keyboard focus indicators across interactive elements. The right-panel icon rail has no text labels or tooltips. Live call controls (mute, hold, end) rely on icon recognition with no accessible text labels. These are WCAG 2.1 AA violations and represent a compliance risk for regulated enterprise clients.
~
Transfer modal — "Reason for Transfer" field absent Moderate
Unlike the reference flow reviewed previously, the 6D transfer modal does not include a free-text reason for transfer field. For compliance and audit purposes, agents should document the context for escalation. This field, with minimum input guidance, strengthens audit traceability.
~
Network Status panel lacks explicit case relevance link Moderate
The Network Status panel surfaces 5 nodes (Mumbai West Tower, Mumbai Central Hub, Navi Mumbai Corridor, Thane Relay Station, Pune Gateway) with health indicators. However, there is no explicit label or visual callout indicating which node is directly associated with this customer's location or the reported roaming event.

Final Conclusion

Assessment verdict — 8.2 / 10

The 6D Technologies Case Management platform has matured meaningfully from the 7.1 baseline. The AI Investigation lifecycle, Live Call with AI Notes, Knowledge Base Playbook, CRM 360° intelligence layer, and the improved Transfer Case modal together constitute a coherent, high-efficiency enterprise workflow that is substantively better than its prior iteration.

The platform is production-ready with known gaps. Three of those gaps — the heading typo, the missing Resolve confirmation, and the absence of a transfer reason field — are low-effort, high-impact fixes that should be closed before any enterprise client demonstration. Accessibility is the one structural investment required for regulated-industry readiness.

With these four actions resolved, this platform is positioned as a reference-quality, AI-augmented enterprise case management experience.
